BADGE(1)                   General Commands                   BADGE(1)

NAME
     badge - Tallyho Badging operator console

SYNOPSIS
     badge                       door states and revoked badges
     badge log                   today's badge events
     badge door DOOR lock|unlock|normal
     badge revoke|restore WHO

DESCRIPTION
     Every change is logged against the operator account. The operator
     account is facilities. The log is reviewed quarterly, by facilities.

NOTES
     People adapt to doors faster than they question them.
