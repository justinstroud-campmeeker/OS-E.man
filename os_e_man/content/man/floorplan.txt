FLOORPLAN(1)               General Commands               FLOORPLAN(1)

NAME
     floorplan, fp - Office Services floor plan, level 3

SYNOPSIS
     floorplan

DESCRIPTION
     Draws level 3 from the live building systems: door modes from
     Tallyho Badging, room bookings from the facilities portal.

     The plan is revision 2019-03. Office Services is confident it is
     still accurate. If the plan and the floor disagree, the plan is
     correct.
