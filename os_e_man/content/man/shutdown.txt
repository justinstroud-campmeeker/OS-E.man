SHUTDOWN(8)                 System Manager's Manual                 SHUTDOWN(8)

NAME
     shutdown - save the world and power it off

SYNOPSIS
     shutdown

DESCRIPTION
     Saves everything and stops the world server. Nothing happens while it
     is off. Start it again with:

          python -m os_e_man

NOTES
     Turning the office off does not make anyone at the office like you
     more. It was worth checking.

SEE ALSO
     quit(1)
