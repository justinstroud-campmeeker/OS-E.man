NAME
     intro - welcome to WKS-0417

DESCRIPTION
     You are Pat Dunn, Consultant II, Retail Delivery, Halvorsen-Pike
     Consulting Group. Your client is Kettering Family Markets. Your desk is
     on the 3rd floor, behind Kevin.

     Everything happens in crab. Start with:
          emmy            read your mail
          train           the mandatory refresher
          dingaling       see what people are saying
          help            everything else

     Time passes: one minute per second while you are connected, slower
     when you are not looking, and slower still after you quit(1). The
     workstation stays up when you disconnect. The workday ends at 17:00,
     but not while you're away; the next day waits for you. You come back
     at 09:00. You always come back.

NOTES
     Some data you will see was not supposed to be there. What you do with
     it is between you and the replica.
