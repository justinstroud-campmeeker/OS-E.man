DINGALING(1)                    General Commands                    DINGALING(1)

NAME
     dingaling - company chat

SYNOPSIS
     dingaling          show the last 20 messages

DESCRIPTION
     The global HPCG chat client. New messages also show up on your
     terminal between prompts, the old-fashioned way. Direct messages
     arrive write(1)-style. If that gets to be too much, see mesg(1).

BUGS
     Your account has been "read-only, pending onboarding" since 2021. You
     can see everything. Nobody can see you. This is filed as a bug.
