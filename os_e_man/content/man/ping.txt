PING(8)                System Manager's Manual                 PING(8)

NAME
     ping - check whether a host is there

SYNOPSIS
     ping HOST

DESCRIPTION
     Sends four packets and reports whether anything answered. Something
     usually does.

NOTES
     IT asks that you do not ping hosts you do not own. IT does not say who
     owns what.

SEE ALSO
     whois(1), netstat(8), /etc/hosts
