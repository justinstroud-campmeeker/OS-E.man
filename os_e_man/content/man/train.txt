NAME
     train - Engineering Excellence Refresher (EER-26)

SYNOPSIS
     train                    progress and current module
     train next               show the current challenge
     train hint               a hint, written by L&D
     train submit [ANSWER]    submit. SQL can go inline; snek opens an editor

DESCRIPTION
     Six modules of SQL and snek, graded automatically by lnd-grader against
     a "sanitized" replica of the client's reporting database.

     Use sql(1) to explore the replica before you submit.

NOTES
     Completion is tracked. Curiosity is not graded.

SEE ALSO
     sql(1), snek(1)
