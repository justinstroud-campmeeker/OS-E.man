NAME
     crab - the HPCG restricted shell

SYNOPSIS
     command [args...] [| grep [-i] PATTERN]

DESCRIPTION
     crab is a shell with the parts people liked removed. It supports
     quoting, exactly one kind of pipe (into grep), history (Up/Down) and
     Tab completion of commands and paths.

     ~ is your home directory. Hidden files start with '.' and are shown
     with ls -a.

BUGS
     Pipes into anything except grep are reported as "a growth mindset
     opportunity".
