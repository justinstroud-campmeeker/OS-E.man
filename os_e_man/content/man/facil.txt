NAME
     facil - HPCG Dunmore facilities portal

SYNOPSIS
     facil status
     facil pods VARIETY
     facil cooler SCHEDULE
     facil order "ITEM"
     facil book ROOM "TITLE"
     facil thermostat DEGF

DESCRIPTION
     Office logistics for the Dunmore office: kitchen pods, water delivery,
     supplies, meeting rooms and the 3rd floor thermostat. Orders go to
     Brightwater Coffee Services and are fulfilled same-day without review.

     Everything done here is attributed to Office Services.

NOTES
     People notice when the office responds to them. They notice more when
     it responds to something they said to only one person.
