QUIT(1)                         General Commands                        QUIT(1)

NAME
     quit, disconnect, exit - close your connection to wks-0417

SYNOPSIS
     quit

DESCRIPTION
     Ends this session and saves. The workstation stays up. The office
     carries on without you: mail arrives, people talk, things get ordered.
     It just happens more slowly when nobody is looking, and the day will
     not end until you come back.

     To actually turn the world off, see shutdown(8).

     Ctrl+D at an empty prompt does the same thing, like any shell.

SEE ALSO
     shutdown(8)
