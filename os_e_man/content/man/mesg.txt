MESG(1)                         General Commands                        MESG(1)

NAME
     mesg - let chat interrupt your terminal, or not

SYNOPSIS
     mesg [y|n]

DESCRIPTION
     With no argument, mesg prints whether chat may write to your terminal:
     "is y" or "is n".

     mesg n stops channel notices and direct messages from appearing
     between your prompts. Nothing is lost: dingaling(1) still has all of
     it. mesg y turns them back on.

NOTES
     Nobody on dingaling can tell whether you have messages turned off. Your
     account is read-only, so nobody on dingaling can tell you exist.

SEE ALSO
     dingaling(1)
